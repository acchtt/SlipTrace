# Football C Researchable-Senior Intake Adjustment

**Status:** ACTIVE PROCEDURE  
**Effective:** 2026-09-30 ICT

## Reason

The unrestricted broad-senior intake admitted too many small/obscure leagues with insufficient current information, increasing Work cost without improving decision quality.

The earlier narrow-core intake had the opposite problem: it could omit meaningful senior blocks such as African international qualifiers.

## New boundary

`RESEARCHABLE_SENIOR_PRODUCTION`

- discover the senior slate broadly;
- hard-exclude non-production categories;
- automatically protect meaningful senior international qualifier/tournament blocks and major continental club competitions;
- apply a cheap current-data researchability gate to ordinary domestic/small competitions;
- admit only when recent team evidence + competition context + a usable mechanism/stat/news layer are available.

Researchability is not a goal-environment filter.

A low-scoring but well-documented league may be admitted.

A high-scoring but obscure weak-data league may be excluded.

## Audit

Track:

`RAW SENIOR -> HARD EXCLUDED -> RESEARCHABILITY EXCLUDED -> ADMITTED`

Audit false exclusions separately from Football C screening false negatives.
