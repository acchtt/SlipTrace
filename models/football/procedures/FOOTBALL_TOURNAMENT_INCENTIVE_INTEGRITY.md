# Football tournament incentive integrity

Applies to the active Football C common evidence state.

Tournament incentive is a mandatory football factor when competition structure
can change scoring behavior. It is not a market-price factor.

## Required factual fields

Where applicable verify:

- competition format and stage;
- qualification/elimination state;
- aggregate state;
- draw utility;
- home/away incentive;
- goal-difference/margin relevance;
- simultaneous-results dependency;
- applicable tiebreak rules.

LIMITED/UNKNOWN evidence never manufactures an expansive/suppressive effect.
It produces INCENTIVE_RECHECK_REQUIRED and provisional football status.

## /rank

A tournament fixture may remain in C research/ranking when incentive details
are unresolved, but the unresolved fields must be explicit. No invented
positive-pressure bonus is allowed.

## /xi

Before a final C BET/WAIT/PASS or strict market authorization on an applicable
fixture:

- tournament_incentive_rechecked=true;
- tournament_incentive_recheck_status=VERIFIED;
- the current evidence epoch contains the resolved facts.

## Effect

Verified incentive may strengthen or suppress only an already identified
football mechanism according to the deterministic C core. It cannot create a
scoring route from nothing.

Early league-phase "win preferred" is not automatically forced chaos.
Tiebreak margin relevance is recorded separately from an actual immediate
margin requirement.

C2/C3/C4 are retired and have no current incentive-policy path.
