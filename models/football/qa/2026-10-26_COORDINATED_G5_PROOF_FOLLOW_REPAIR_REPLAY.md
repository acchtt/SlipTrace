## Coordinated code change

1. **G5/G6 independent source-proof gate**: preserve existing independently funded G3/G4 paths. An active C G5 requires a specifically sourced, additional current fifth-goal route. Required fields are `goal5_fifth_goal_route_verified`, `goal5_fifth_goal_route_basis` and nonempty `goal5_fifth_goal_source_refs`. Missing legacy proof fails closed. This prevents general HIGH dimensions or reused G4 evidence alone from silently authorizing O4.50; G6 inherits the G5 gate. The affirmative proof must predate the price and kickoff and have independently checkable source references.
2. **FOLLOW allocator rollback**: retain the validated zero-authority CONTROL_REVIEW exclusion; restore chronological protection of primary CLEARABILITY nominations by exact kickoff, with quality comparison inside a kickoff and global BALANCED/UPSIDE secondary allocation. The prior global-primary clearability implementation failed the full multi-board certificate-clear replay, despite improving raw workload scores.
3. **G3 pathways unchanged**: the asymmetric carrier/two-sided challengers A–D in the prior report failed the multi-day promotion gate. No recall-destructive carrier blanket veto or outcome-derived threshold has been introduced.
