# Football C — selection-inversion coordinated repair

**Date:** 2026-10-24 ICT

**Authority:** Football C only

**Workflow:** `/sweep -> /rank -> /xi -> /audit`
**Change class:** architectural authority/coverage/execution repair; no
result-fitted threshold change

## Root cause

The retained evidence does not prove that Football C is globally inverted. It
does prove that the production chain contained independent recall and authority
holes:

1. a cheap recent-history screen could terminate research before C;
2. researcher input, rather than C, owned board state;
3. certificate depth preceded burden-normalized clearability in rank;
4. one endpoint label mixed G3 failure with G4+ continuation risk;
5. `/xi` evaluated one screenshot quote instead of the full visible ladder;
6. G4 MIXED, G3-only O3.25 BOOK, and upstream PARTIAL-rank rules conflicted;
7. screened rows, PASSes, exceptions, BOOKs, and ACTs lacked one prospective
   denominator.

The strongest settled signal remains the protected G3/O2.50 layer. This repair
therefore restores authority and observability without reversing PASS to ACT or
fitting yesterday's scores.

## Implemented rules

- `/sweep` retains GOAL_PRIORITY then GOAL_REVIEW and admits at most four
  quantified negative screens to `CONTROL_REVIEW`. The fixed capacity is a
  workload bound. Ordering uses only frozen cheap-filter importance, carrier
  route, mean 3+/4+ rates, draw-trap rate, screen score, kickoff, and match ID.
  CONTROL_REVIEW itself has no certificate, state, or execution authority.
- Football C compiles production state: funded G3 -> FOCUS; direct material
  suppression/route destruction without funding -> PASS; other researched
  no-certificate rows -> WATCH/REVIEW_ONLY. Declared state and mismatch remain
  audit metadata.
- G3 endpoint pressure and upper-tail pressure are separate. G3 hard controls
  still block the floor; tail-only suppression may lower the direct ceiling to
  O2.50 but cannot erase an independently funded G3.
- `/xi` validates every explicitly visible screenshot line/price, evaluates
  each through the same C policy, selects the highest executable supported
  visible line, and persists that selection. It performs zero odds searches
  and infers zero prices.
- G4 terminal completeness is FUNDED or REJECTED. Mixed path diagnostics remain
  visible. Historical COMPLETE_MIXED payloads normalize to REJECTED without
  mutating their declared status.
- G3-only BOOK is O2.75 or O3.00 only. O3.25 is removed; O3.50 requires G4.
- Upstream PARTIAL sweep remains unrankable. Downstream rank-research
  checkpointing remains distinct and historically readable.
- `football-c-calibration-v1` records every source-visible prematch fixture with
  zero selection/learning authority. Settlement is append-only and the audit
  compiler reports the universal execution, PASS, screen, ranking, distribution,
  and exception metrics.
- A clearability-first ranking challenger is emitted for replay only. The
  production champion ranking is unchanged because settled per-fixture evidence
  is insufficient for promotion.

## Thresholds deliberately unchanged

- all existing GOAL_PRIORITY/GOAL_REVIEW admission thresholds;
- distributed-G3 hardening and all G3/G4/G5/G6 funding predicates;
- material low-draw G3 hard-control threshold `>= 0.25`;
- supported-price minimum 1.65 and the existing 1.60–1.64 soft-zone contract;
- O2.75 BOOK floor 1.85 and O3.00 BOOK floor 1.90;
- tournament, H2H, competition-scope, friendlies/Africa/Latvia, XI, source,
  chronology, and persistence gates.

The only new numeric capacity is four CONTROL_REVIEW rows per sweep. It is not
a football threshold and was not chosen from results. The obsolete O3.25 BOOK
floor was deleted rather than tuned.

## Execution/PASS coverage

The rank replay has no screenshot ladders and therefore cannot recreate ACT,
BOOK, or priced PASS decisions. It reports ACT-*capable* football coverage only.
The primary audit's legacy execution cohorts remain audit facts:

- priced PASS: 9, with 4 correct rejects and 5 false negatives (55.6%);
- legacy deterministic ACT: 2 wins, 3 losses, 1 half-loss.

No missing screenshot line/price was inferred, so repaired retrospective PASS
coverage and “lower supported visible line” counts are intentionally not
fabricated. The universal prospective ledger now captures those fields for all
future decisions.
