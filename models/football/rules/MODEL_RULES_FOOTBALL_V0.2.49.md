# Football v0.2.49 — Two-Sided Priority Patch

**Status:** ACTIVE OFFICIAL PATCH  
**Effective:** 2026-09-08 ICT  
**Base:** Football v0.2.47 CLEAN  
**Shadow:** Football v0.2.48-SHADOW continues in parallel after receiving the same evidence state  

This file is a narrow official ranking patch. It does not rewrite historical v0.2.47 decisions and does not revive the archived Sep 1 hardening framework.

---

## 1. Purpose

Restore the intended structural hierarchy: **genuine two-sided goal environments are the default priority over carrier-led environments when the grades are comparable.**

The elite-carrier exception remains valid, but it is an exception rather than a peer-by-default shortcut to the top of the board.

---

## 2. Structural archetype priority

When two candidates have the same PRE grade, or are close enough in structural quality that the board order requires a tiebreak, rank the archetypes in this order:

`TWO-SIDED > ELITE CARRIER > CARRIER-LED > FRAGILE / OTHER`

This archetype priority is part of **STRUCTURAL QUALITY** and is applied before carrier ceiling is used to break a tie.

A two-sided label is valid only when **both teams have credible independent scoring routes**. Mere defensive leakage, recent high totals, or one opponent occasionally scoring does not make a match genuinely two-sided.

---

## 3. Elite-carrier exception

An ELITE CARRIER may rank above a genuine same-grade TWO-SIDED candidate only when the carrier case is **clearly superior on both**:

1. repeatable chance quality / self-funding 3+ capacity; and
2. failure-mode resistance.

Carrier reputation, raw GF, recent 3+/4+ scorelines, or a high theoretical ceiling alone are not sufficient to leapfrog a genuine two-sided profile.

If suppression resistance, weak secondary contribution, or a realistic 1-0 / 2-0 / 0-1 / 0-2 type failure mode remains material, the elite-carrier exception does not override the two-sided priority.

---

## 4. Board and research priority

For FOCUS/WATCHLIST ordering and same-window attention:

- compare grade first;
- within the same grade, apply `TWO-SIDED > ELITE CARRIER > CARRIER-LED > FRAGILE / OTHER`;
- give the stronger two-sided candidate first access to deeper research, XI review, and market pricing;
- do not promote a B+ two-sided match to A2 merely because of archetype;
- do not demote a genuinely superior elite carrier merely to satisfy the ordering mechanically.

The hierarchy determines **priority**, not automatic LOCK status.

---

## 5. XI and market stages

Confirmed XI and market still rerank the same surviving candidates.

At XI:

- preserve the frozen PRE archetype;
- verify that both independent routes remain credible for TWO-SIDED profiles;
- verify that the elite carrier still has self-funding capacity and acceptable resistance if using the exception.

At market:

- price cannot rescue a structurally weaker archetype;
- protected lines remain preferred when they preserve the thesis;
- a carrier-led match must not leapfrog a stronger two-sided match simply because its offered price looks attractive.

---

## 6. Interaction with v0.2.48-SHADOW

Both tracks receive the same structural ordering and evidence state.

The shadow model then applies its existing explicit deltas, including carrier hardening and no-rescue logic.

A shadow HOLD does not suppress an official v0.2.49 decision, and an official HOLD does not erase the shadow result.
