# KRXC vs BFXY — 2026-08-25 Afternoon Calibration Review

## Purpose

Post-slate calibration review for KRX Challengers vs BNK FEARX Youth, preserving historical verdicts while extracting process lessons for future authority epochs.

## Confirmed lessons

### A. Small kill-handicap asymmetry

A small underdog `+H` can be mathematically positive while still being structurally poor protection. In Game 3, KRX +1.5 was initially promoted too aggressively. The user correctly rejected it as too small. The lesson is not that small handicaps are universally bad: a small favorite `-H` can still be an efficient expression of a favorite edge. The structural problem is usually small underdog cushion that behaves too much like ML and can be erased by one normal late fight.

### B. Professional meta layer

Draft reading should distinguish professional meta priority from solo-queue strength. Relevant pro variables include pick/ban priority on the competition patch, blind safety, flex value, system fit, pairing dependency, player stage comfort, and whether a champion supplies a scarce archetype in Fearless.

### C. Archetype inventory in Fearless

Functional depletion matters more than raw champion count. Repeatedly consuming reliable engage junglers/supports, waveclear, front line, or carry-protection options is a series-pool event even if many legal champions remain.

### D. Draft causal order

The most useful pro draft read follows lane states and access into first move, objective sequence, transition quality, 2/3-item fight shape, WCSR, execution burden, and series adaptation. Champion labels alone are insufficient.

### E. Screenshot synchronization

If the user explicitly confirms paired market and scoreboard screenshots are the same state, phone status-bar capture times alone should not invalidate synchronization. Internal state contradictions still matter.

### F. Recent workload

Playing the previous day belongs in execution context, not as a standalone fatigue fade. It requires a mechanism before materially moving a verdict.

### G. Duration Over

Game 4 validated the existing causal DOVC framework: BFX obtained early objectives but repeatedly failed to convert into structures, and the map reached 34:36. The result supports, but does not replace, the rule that low tower count or low kills alone are neutral; observed failed conversion/reset mechanisms are required.

## Historical preservation

Do not rewrite prior slate predictions, positions, or P/L under these new rules. Apply the calibration prospectively after canonical activation/relock.
