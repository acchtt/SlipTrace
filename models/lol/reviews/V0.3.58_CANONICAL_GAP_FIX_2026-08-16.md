## Decision

Remain on **LoL v0.3.58**. Do not restore v0.3.59 or v0.3.60 as active model layers.

The daily audit showed that the canonical v0.3.58 reset had already incorporated the KRX vs BRO Duration Over / Structure Substitution correction, but it was still missing several narrow, mechanism-specific controls that had been developed during the retired later-version audit.

Those useful controls are now re-authorized and incorporated directly into v0.3.58 itself:

1. Total Kills Under — **Forward Regime Persistence (FRP)**;
2. Duration Under — **Fast-Close Robustness (FCR)** and one-disruption stress test;
3. Live Moneyline — **Lead Decomposition** and **Compulsory Fight Control (CFC)**;
4. **Same-Mechanism Re-certification (SMR)** for repeated same-series theses after a diagnosed failure.

This is not a version promotion and is not a blanket tightening after losses.

## Why these controls are needed

### Total Kills Under
Several losses came from treating a quiet observed interval as if it proved future quiet. The missing question was whether the next compulsory objective/contact cycle would be at least as suppressible as the interval already observed.

### Duration Under
A shortest plausible fast-close route can exist without being robust. If one ordinary reset, partial conversion, waveclear cycle or traded objective destroys the Under, the route is too fragile for a normal TAKE.

### Live Moneyline
Modest gold/CS/objective leads were repeatedly upgraded into win-probability leads even when the opponent retained easier neutral-setup first contact, re-engage, protected DPS continuation and objective-turn control. Resource lead must be separated from control lead.

### Same-series repetition
A previous loss never creates a blanket veto. But once its failure mechanism is diagnosed, a later same-series TAKE that relies on materially the same mechanism must show what actually changed.

## Boundaries
