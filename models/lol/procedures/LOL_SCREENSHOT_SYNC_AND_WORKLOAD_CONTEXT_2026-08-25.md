# Screenshot Synchronization + Workload Context Clarification — 2026-08-25

## Screenshot synchronization

When the user explicitly confirms that a scoreboard screenshot and market screenshot represent the same live state, phone/device status-bar timestamps or capture-order differences do not by themselves invalidate synchronization.

Reject the pair only when internal state evidence materially contradicts synchronization, such as incompatible game/map identity, impossible clock progression, or state/market evidence clearly belonging to different maps.

This does not waive the two-snapshot requirement for live ML/KH/TK.

## Recent workload

Previous-day play, quick turnaround, travel, or long-series workload may be logged as professional execution context but is not a standalone fade or TAKE signal. It may matter only when attached to an observable mechanism such as preparation compression, roster/substitution constraint, repeated late-series degradation, or verified schedule stress.
